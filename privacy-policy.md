With your explicit permission, we may access your device's precise location in order to:

When publishing a listing, you voluntarily provide the location of your garage sale. This location becomes part of the publicly visible listing and is available to other users of the Service. You control the location you publish and may edit or remove it by editing or deleting your listing.
